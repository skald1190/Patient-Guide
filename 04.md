# Section 4: Trigeminal neuralgia (face pain)

### About this condition

Trigeminal neuralgia causes sudden, brief, **electric-shock-like pain on one side of the face.** Light touch, chewing, talking, brushing your teeth, or a breeze can set it off. It is often caused by a blood vessel pressing on the main nerve of the face, so we usually order an MRI to look for a cause. The pain is not dangerous, but it can be severe. Medicines that calm the nerve work well for most people.

Medicines and treatments in this section: carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine, nerve stimulation devices (optional add-on).

## Trigeminal neuralgia plan {#trigeminal-neuralgia-plan}

- **Daily preventive medicine:** carbamazepine or oxcarbazepine (below). If one does not work or causes side effects, we try the other or add another medicine.

- **During a bad flare:** call us. We may raise your dose or offer a numbing injection.

- **Go to the ER if:** pain keeps you from eating or drinking. Bring this guide.

- **Opioid pain pills do not work** for this type of pain.

- **Pain gone for a while?** We may lower your medicine slowly. **Never stop suddenly.** If pain comes back, go back to your last dose and call us.

- **If medicines are not working:** surgery or other procedures can help. We will refer you if needed.

- [ ] **Nerve stimulation device (optional add-on):** some people try a TENS unit or the Cefaly forehead device. My device: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

- These devices are **not FDA-cleared for trigeminal neuralgia**, and the research is still early. They may help some people need less medicine.

- They **do not replace your daily medicine.** Do not lower your medicine unless we tell you to.

- Cefaly reaches only the forehead nerve, so it is most likely to help if your pain is in the forehead or around the eye.

- If touching your face sets off pain, a device on the skin can set off an attack. **Stop using it if your pain gets worse.**

## Carbamazepine (Tegretol) {#carbamazepine}

Before starting: blood tests, plus a genetic test if you have Asian ancestry.

You will add a little every 3 days until your pain is controlled. **Stop going up once your pain is controlled.** Example if adding 100 mg every 3 days:

|-----------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| 1 to 3    | 100 mg      | 100 mg      | ☐        |
| 4 to 6    | 100 mg      | 200 mg      | ☐        |
| 7 to 9    | 200 mg      | 200 mg      | ☐        |
| 10 to 12  | 200 mg      | 300 mg      | ☐        |
| 13 and on | 300 mg      | 300 mg      | ☐        |

My highest dose (do not go above): \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ mg a day.

- **Common side effects:** dizziness, sleepiness, unsteady walking, double vision, nausea. If these happen, go back to the last dose that felt OK and call us.

- **Call us right away if:** you have a rash, fever, mouth sores, sore throat, easy bruising, or confusion.

- **Interactions:** carbamazepine makes **birth control and many other medicines work less well**, including blood thinners. See **A3, Alerts 1 and 2**.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us (some increased risk of birth defects, especially at higher doses) · **Breastfeeding:** ✅ Generally OK

## Oxcarbazepine (Trileptal) {#oxcarbazepine}

Before starting: a sodium blood test, plus a genetic test if you have Asian ancestry.

#### Stop going up once your pain is controlled.

| **Days**                     | **Morning** | **Evening** | **Done** |
|------------------------------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| 1 to 3                       | 150 mg      | 150 mg      | ☐        |
| 4 to 6                       | 300 mg      | 300 mg      | ☐        |
| 7 to 9 (if still in pain)    | 450 mg      | 450 mg      | ☐        |
| 10 and on (if still in pain) | 600 mg      | 600 mg      | ☐        |

- **Common side effects:** dizziness, sleepiness, double vision, headache, nausea.

- **Call us if:** you have a rash, confusion, or unusual tiredness or headache. These can be signs of low sodium.

- **Interactions:** oxcarbazepine makes **birth control work less well.** See **A3, Alert 1**.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ✅ Generally OK

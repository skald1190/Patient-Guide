# Section 16: Sleep and restless legs

### About these conditions

- **Restless legs syndrome:** an uncomfortable urge to move your legs, usually in the evening or at night, that gets better when you move. **Low iron is a common, treatable cause**, so we check iron levels first. Restless legs is also common during pregnancy.

- **Insomnia:** trouble falling asleep or staying asleep. It is common with neurologic conditions, pain, mood problems, and some medicines. Good sleep habits and therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) come first. Sleep medicines work best at the lowest dose for the shortest time.

Medicines and treatments in this section: iron, leg compression device, Nidra, ropinirole (Requip), eszopiclone (Lunesta), low-dose doxepin (Silenor), ramelteon (Rozerem), temazepam (Restoril), Belsomra, Dayvigo, mirtazapine (Remeron).

### Things that can make restless legs worse {applies=iron-rls,leg-compression-device,nidra,ropinirole,low-dose-opioid}

- Alcohol and caffeine.

- Sleep aids and allergy pills with diphenhydramine (Benadryl, Tylenol PM, ZzzQuil).

- Some antidepressants and some nausea medicines. **Mirtazapine can make restless legs worse** in some people.

- **Do not stop a prescribed medicine on your own.** Ask us first.

## Iron for restless legs (step 1 for everyone) {#iron-rls}

Low iron in the brain can cause restless legs, even when your blood count is normal. **For restless legs, we aim for a higher iron level than most doctors use.**

#### IV iron (iron through a vein)

We may give you iron by IV if your iron saturation is **below 45%** AND your ferritin is **below 100**, and one of these is true:

- iron pills upset your stomach too much,

- your body cannot absorb iron pills well (for example, after weight-loss surgery or with certain stomach, bowel, or inflammatory conditions),

- your restless legs have not improved after a fair trial of iron pills, or

- you need faster relief than iron pills can give.

IV iron is given in an infusion center, usually as 1 or 2 visits. **If you are pregnant, IV iron is usually given only after the first trimester.**

### Treatments without pills

## Leg compression device (pneumatic compression) {#leg-compression-device}

- Soft sleeves wrap around your legs and gently squeeze and release.

- **Use it every day, before the time your symptoms usually start** (for example, in the early evening).

- **Side effects:** usually none.

- We send an order for the device. Insurance coverage varies.

- **Pregnancy:** ✅ Generally OK · **Breastfeeding:** ✅ Generally OK

## Nidra (leg nerve stimulation device) {#nidra}

- A band worn on each lower leg, just below the knee. It sends gentle pulses to a nerve there to calm restless legs. **Prescription needed.**

- For restless legs that are not controlled well enough with medicine. You can use it with your medicine.

- **How to use:** at bedtime or when symptoms start, as shown in your device training. My plan: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

- **Side effects:** mild discomfort or skin irritation where the band sits. This usually gets better with time.

- **Good to know:** it works only while you keep using it. Some people are able to lower their restless legs medicine over time, **but only with our OK.**

- **A store-bought TENS unit is not the same as Nidra.** It may help some people for a short time.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ✅ Generally OK

## Ropinirole (Requip) {#ropinirole}

Take 1 to 3 hours before bedtime.

| **Days**                         | **Dose**        | **Done** |
|----------------------------------|-----------------|----------|
| 1 and 2                          | 0.25 mg         | ☐        |
| 3 to 7                           | 0.5 mg          | ☐        |
| Week 2                           | 1 mg            | ☐        |
| Later (only if your doctor says) | \_\_\_\_\_\_ mg | ☐        |

- **Before starting:** iron blood tests. Low iron makes restless legs worse.

- **Watch for "augmentation":** symptoms starting earlier in the day, feeling stronger, or spreading to your arms. **Call us. Do not raise the dose yourself.** A higher dose makes this worse.

- **Side effects:** nausea, dizziness, sudden sleepiness, new urges (gambling, shopping).

- **Stopping:** we lower it slowly.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ⛔ **Avoid** (lowers milk supply)

## Low-dose opioid (methadone, tramadol, or another opioid) for severe restless legs {#low-dose-opioid}

- For restless legs that are still severe after iron and other treatments, or when another medicine stopped working or made symptoms worse (augmentation).

- **Restless legs doses are low**, much lower than doses used for pain.

- Full instructions, safety rules, and the dementia question are in **Section 17B**.

### Rules for all sleep medicines {applies=eszopiclone,doxepin-low-dose,ramelteon,temazepam,belsomra,dayvigo,mirtazapine}

- [ ] **Take only when you can stay in bed 7 to 8 hours.**

- [ ] No alcohol or other sedating medicines.

- [ ] **Do not drive** if you still feel drowsy in the morning.

- [ ] **Therapy for insomnia (CBT-I)** works as well as or better than pills over time. We recommend it for everyone.

- [ ] **Pregnant or breastfeeding?** Most sleep medicines have little safety information. CBT-I and good sleep habits are the safest first step. Talk to us before using any sleep medicine.

## Eszopiclone (Lunesta) {#eszopiclone}

- **How to take:** start 1 mg right before bed. Do not take it after a heavy meal.

- **Stop and call us** if you sleepwalk, sleep-drive, or do things at night you do not remember.

- **Side effects:** bitter or metallic taste, next-day grogginess.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ⚠️ Discuss with us

## Doxepin, low dose (Silenor) {#doxepin-low-dose}

- **How to take:** start 6 mg (3 mg if 65 or older) within 30 minutes of bed. Do not take it within 3 hours of a meal.

- **Side effects:** daytime sleepiness.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ⛔ **Avoid** (can cause dangerous sleepiness and breathing problems in babies)

## Ramelteon (Rozerem) {#ramelteon}

- **How to take:** 8 mg within 30 minutes of bed. Do not take it after a high-fat meal.

- **Side effects:** dizziness, tiredness. Not habit-forming.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ⚠️ Discuss with us

## Temazepam (Restoril) {#temazepam}

- **How to take:** start 7.5 mg at bedtime. For short-term use only.

- See **Section 17: Benzodiazepine safety.**

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ⚠️ Discuss with us

## Belsomra (suvorexant) {#belsomra}

- **How to take:** start 10 mg within 30 minutes of bed, with at least 7 hours before you need to wake up.

- **Side effects:** next-day sleepiness, briefly feeling unable to move when falling asleep or waking (harmless), vivid dreams.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ⚠️ Discuss with us

## Dayvigo (lemborexant) {#dayvigo}

- **How to take:** start 5 mg right before bed, with at least 7 hours before you need to wake up.

- **Side effects:** same as Belsomra.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ⚠️ Discuss with us

## Mirtazapine (Remeron) (for sleep, low mood, or both) {#mirtazapine}

- **How to take:** once a day at bedtime. My dose: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

- **Good to know:** lower doses are often **more** sleep-inducing than higher doses. For low mood, it takes 4 to 6 weeks to work fully. It can also help a poor appetite.

- **Common side effects:** bigger appetite and weight gain, morning grogginess, dry mouth, dizziness.

- **Call us if:** you have new or worse depression, thoughts of harming yourself, or a **fever, sore throat, or mouth sores** (rarely, it can lower white blood cells). Also tell us if your restless legs get worse.

- **Avoid:** combining with other mood medicines, tramadol, or triptans unless we know about it (risk of serotonin syndrome). Avoid alcohol.

- **Do not stop suddenly.** We lower it slowly.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ✅ Generally OK

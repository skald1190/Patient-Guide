# Section 10: ALS

### About this condition

**ALS (amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, or Lou Gehrig's disease)** is a condition in which the nerve cells that control muscles slowly stop working. This causes muscle weakness that usually starts in one area, such as a hand, a foot, or the muscles for speaking and swallowing, and spreads over time. Many people also have cramps, stiffness, tiredness, weight loss, and changes in mood or sleep. There is no cure yet. **Riluzole and edaravone can modestly slow the disease.** Care from a team of doctors, nurses, therapists, dietitians, and social workers helps people live better and longer. Much of this section is about comfort, breathing, and quality of life.

Medicines and care in this section: riluzole, edaravone (Radicava ORS), saliva medicines, Nuedexta, breathing care, mood and anxiety care.

Also see: Section 9 (stiffness and cramps).

## Edaravone oral liquid (Radicava ORS) {#edaravone}

- **How to take:** 5 mL once a day in the morning, on an empty stomach after an overnight fast.

- Shake the bottle for 30 seconds and use the syringe that comes with it.

- **Only water for 1 hour after each dose.**

- **Feeding tube:** flush with 30 mL of water before and after.

- Throw out the bottle 15 days after opening.

| **Cycle**         | **Days taking it**             | **Then**    | **Done** |
|-------------------|--------------------------------|-------------|----------|
| Cycle 1           | Every day for 14 days          | 14 days off | ☐        |
| Cycle 2           | 10 days out of a 14-day window | 14 days off | ☐        |
| Cycle 3           | 10 days out of a 14-day window | 14 days off | ☐        |
| Every cycle after | 10 days out of a 14-day window | 14 days off | ☐        |

- **Go to the ER if:** you have hives, lip or face swelling, wheezing, or trouble breathing. Edaravone contains a sulfite that can trigger allergic or asthma reactions.

- **Common side effects:** bruising, unsteady walking, headache, tiredness.

## Breathing care in ALS {#breathing-care-in-als}

- **At each visit**, we check your breathing strength.

- **Tell us if you have:** shortness of breath, trouble lying flat, morning headaches, daytime sleepiness, waking often at night, or a weak cough.

- [ ] **BiPAP (breathing machine with a mask):** start using it at night, and add daytime use if it helps.

- People who start earlier and use it more hours a day tend to live longer.

- A respiratory therapist will fit your mask. Bring your machine data card or app to visits.

- [ ] **Cough assist machine:** \_\_\_\_\_\_ times a day. Use it more often when you have a cold.

- [ ] **Home suction machine.**

- **Do not use oxygen alone** unless we prescribe it. It can hide breathing problems.

- **Go to the ER if:** breathing gets worse, or you have new confusion or extreme sleepiness, or a fever with thick mucus.

# Healthy habits for your brain and nerves

## Sleep

- Keep a consistent sleep schedule. Get up at the same time every day, even on weekends or during vacations.
- Set a bedtime that is early enough for you to get at least 7 hours of sleep.
- Don't go to bed unless you are sleepy.
- If you don't fall asleep after 20 minutes, get out of bed.
- Establish a relaxing bedtime routine.
- Use your bed only for sleep and sex.
- Make your bedroom quiet and relaxing. Keep the room at a comfortable, cool temperature.
- Limit exposure to bright light in the evenings.
- Turn off electronic devices at least 30 minutes before bedtime. You could try reading a relaxing book or listening to a relaxing podcast before bed. For podcast recommendations, I would recommend "Nothing Much Happens".
- Don't eat a large meal before bedtime. If you are hungry at night, eat a light, healthy snack.
- Exercise regularly and maintain a healthy diet. I would recommend exercising in the morning. If you need to exercise before bed, do so about 3 hours before bedtime and avoid strenuous workouts, focusing on mild to moderate cardio.
- Avoid consuming caffeine in the late afternoon or evening.
- Avoid consuming alcohol before bedtime.
- Reduce your fluid intake before bedtime.
- Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for insomnia (CBT-I): [veterantraining.va.gov/insomnia](https://www.veterantraining.va.gov/insomnia/)

**Supplements for sleep:**

- Magnesium glycinate 400 mg
- L-theanine 200 mg
- Low-dose melatonin 0.3 mg
- Chamomile tea

## Exercise

- Start exercising with a goal of 30 to 60 minutes, 3 to 5 times a week.
- Start small (even 5 minutes once a week!).
- Slowly increase exercise duration and frequency to make it a habit. Choose an activity you enjoy, since no one type of exercise is best.

## Eat

- There is no single "neurological diet", but you may try the [Mediterranean diet](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/16037-mediterranean-diet).
- Eat healthy, well-balanced meals at least 3 times a day.
- Avoid skipping meals.
- Recommended fluid intake: about 15.5 cups (3.7 liters) of fluids a day for men. About 11.5 cups (2.7 liters) of fluids a day for women.
- Stop caffeine or consistently use less than 200 mg a day (1 to 2 cups of brewed coffee).
- Aim for optimal weight (body mass index 18.5 to 25 kg/m²).

## Stress

To help manage your symptoms, consider learning the following techniques from books, apps, or a psychologist:

- Cognitive behavioral therapy
- Mindfulness
- Biofeedback
- Relaxation techniques like visualization or progressive muscle relaxation

## Why we use this agreement

This agreement is a shared plan, not a contract and not a sign that we don't trust you. Medicines like opioids (methadone, tramadol, oxycodone, buprenorphine) and benzodiazepines (diazepam/Valium, clonazepam/Klonopin) can help a lot. They can also cause serious harm if not used carefully. This agreement keeps you safe and protects your access to the medicine.

## I understand the risks (check each box)

- [ ] Sleepiness, slower thinking, and falls. I will not drive until I know how the medicine affects me.
- [ ] Constipation (opioids). I will follow the bowel plan in my guide.
- [ ] Physical dependence is normal after regular use. It is not the same as addiction. Stopping suddenly can cause withdrawal. Benzodiazepines can also cause seizures if stopped suddenly.
- [ ] Addiction can happen, although it is uncommon at the low doses we use.
- [ ] Overdose and slowed breathing. The risk is much higher if I mix this medicine with alcohol, other opioids, benzodiazepines, sleeping pills, gabapentin/pregabalin, or muscle relaxers. The risk is also higher with untreated sleep apnea.
- [ ] Pregnancy: I will tell the office right away if I am pregnant or planning pregnancy.

## My part

- [ ] I will get this medicine only from this office and fill it at one pharmacy.
- [ ] I will take it exactly as prescribed and will not change my dose without talking to the office first.
- [ ] No early refills. I will request refills \_\_\_\_\_\_ business days ahead. Lost, stolen, or used-up medicine usually cannot be replaced early.
- [ ] I will store it locked up, never share or sell it, and take leftover medicine to a pharmacy take-back bin.
- [ ] I will do urine drug tests or pill counts when asked. These are routine for everyone on these medicines.
- [ ] I will keep my visits, at least every \_\_\_\_\_\_ months.
- [ ] I will tell the office about new medicines, ER visits, surgery, pregnancy, alcohol or cannabis use, or other prescriptions for controlled medicines.
- [ ] Naloxone (Narcan): I will keep it at home, and someone I live with will know where it is and how to use it.

## Our part (your care team)

- ✔ We check the Arizona prescription monitoring database (CSPMP), as all prescribers do.
- ✔ We review together whether the medicine is helping and is safe.
- ✔ If a test result is unexpected, we will talk with you first. Tests can sometimes be wrong.
- ✔ We will not stop your medicine suddenly without talking with you, except in an emergency or if there is a safety threat.
- ✔ If the medicine is not helping or not safe, we will taper it slowly and work on other options with you.
- ✔ If addiction develops, we will help you get treatment. We will not abandon your care.

## When the plan may change

The plan may change if the medicine stops helping, side effects outweigh the benefit, or use becomes unsafe (for example, early refills, prescriptions from other doctors, unexpected drug tests, or sharing medicine). In those cases we will meet, talk it through, and adjust the plan together.

We review and re-sign this agreement every year, or sooner if your medicine changes.

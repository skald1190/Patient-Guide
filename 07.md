# Section 7: Memory and thinking (dementia)

### About this condition

**Dementia** means memory and thinking problems that are serious enough to affect daily life. Alzheimer's disease is the most common cause. Others include Lewy body disease, Parkinson's disease, and damage from small strokes.

- **Donepezil, rivastigmine, and memantine** can help memory and daily function for a while. They do not stop the disease.

- **Lecanemab and donanemab** work differently. They clear a protein called amyloid from the brain and can slow decline in **early** Alzheimer's disease. They need regular MRIs for safety.

- Some people develop **hallucinations, agitation, or confusion.** Other approaches are tried first, and medicines are used carefully when needed.

Medicines in this section: donepezil, memantine, rivastigmine, quetiapine, pimavanserin, aripiprazole, brexpiprazole, lecanemab (Leqembi), donanemab (Kisunla).

Also see: Section 18 (supplements: methyl B12 and methylfolate).

**Pregnancy and breastfeeding:** most medicines in this section have little or no safety information in pregnancy or breastfeeding. If this applies to you, talk to us.

## Rivastigmine (Exelon) {#rivastigmine}

Patch schedule:

| **Step**                                          | **Patch**                 | **Done** |
|---------------------------------------------------|---------------------------|----------|
| At least 4 weeks                                  | 4.6 mg patch, once a day  | ☐        |
| Next (if doing OK)                                | 9.5 mg patch, once a day  | ☐        |
| After at least 4 more weeks (if your doctor says) | 13.3 mg patch, once a day | ☐        |

- **Patch tips:** take off the old patch before putting on a new one. **Never wear more than one patch.** Change the spot each day: back, chest, or upper arm.

- **Capsules instead of patch:** start 1.5 mg twice a day with food. Go up by 1.5 mg per dose every 2 weeks if your doctor says, up to 6 mg twice a day.

- **Missed more than 3 days? Do not restart at your usual dose.** Call us. You need to start back at the lowest dose.

- **Side effects:** nausea, vomiting, less appetite, weight loss, skin irritation under the patch.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ⚠️ Discuss with us

## Lecanemab (Leqembi) {#lecanemab}

- **What happens:** a 1-hour IV infusion **every 2 weeks.**

- **MRI checklist (required for safety):**

- [ ] Before starting

- [ ] Before infusion 5

- [ ] Before infusion 7

- [ ] Before infusion 14

- [ ] Anytime new symptoms appear

- **Brain swelling or small bleeds (ARIA)** can happen. They usually cause no symptoms and are found on MRI.

- **Call us right away if:** you have a new headache, confusion, vision changes, dizziness, nausea, or a seizure.

- **ER or stroke team:** always tell them you take this medicine. Some clot-busting drugs can cause bleeding with it.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us (not studied) · **Breastfeeding:** ⚠️ Discuss with us (not studied)

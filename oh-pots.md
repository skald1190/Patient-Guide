# Extra steps for orthostatic hypotension and POTS

These steps are for blood pressure drops when standing (orthostatic hypotension, including the neurally mediated type) and POTS. They add to your regular lifestyle plan.

⚠️ **Check with us first if you have heart failure, kidney disease, high blood pressure (especially when lying down), or leg swelling.** Extra salt and fluid may not be safe for you.

## Fluids

Drink the same amount of fluid as the regular plan:

- **Men:** about 15.5 cups (3.7 liters, about 125 oz) a day
- **Women:** about 11.5 cups (2.7 liters, about 90 oz) a day

## Salt

**Add 5 to 10 grams of salt to your day.**

2 grams of salt is about **⅓ teaspoon** of table salt.

| Salt | Table salt | Sodium (what food labels show) |
|------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| 2 grams | ⅓ teaspoon | about 800 mg |
| 4 grams | ⅔ teaspoon | about 1,600 mg |
| 6 grams | 1 teaspoon | about 2,400 mg |
| 8 grams | 1⅓ teaspoons | about 3,200 mg |
| 10 grams | 1⅔ teaspoons | about 4,000 mg |

So your goal of 5 to 10 grams is a little less than **1 teaspoon** up to about **1⅔ teaspoons** of table salt a day. A 1-gram salt tablet counts as 1 gram of salt.

## Compression stockings

- Wear compression stockings **during the day**.
- **Take them off at night.** You do not need them while lying down.

## Where these recommendations come from

The American College of Cardiology recommends increased salt and fluid intake as reasonable in selected patients with neurogenic orthostatic hypotension (Class IIb, C-LD). A typical target is 6 to 9 g of salt per day (100 to 150 mmol, about 1 to 2 teaspoons). [1]

- **How it works:** salt expands plasma volume. Patients who already eat a lot of salt get little extra benefit. Drinking water raises blood pressure through a sympathetic pressor effect that peaks about 30 minutes after drinking. [1]
- **Caution:** extra salt and fluid may not help patients with high blood pressure, kidney disease, heart failure, or other heart problems. Long-term benefits and risks are unknown. [1]
- **Water bolus as a rescue measure:** drinking 500 mL (about 2 cups) of water over 3 to 4 minutes raises blood pressure by about 24/12 mm Hg in orthostatic hypotension. The effect peaks at about 35 minutes and lasts up to 60 minutes. It is useful in the morning or before meals. [1]
- **High blood pressure:** the American Heart Association advises caution, because salt loading can worsen high blood pressure when lying down. For a patient with high blood pressure or coronary artery disease, compression garments or an abdominal binder raise standing blood pressure without worsening lying-down blood pressure, so they may be the safer first step. [1]

[1] Shen WK, Sheldon RS, Benditt DG, et al. 2017 ACC/AHA/HRS Guideline for the Evaluation and Management of Patients With Syncope. *Circulation*. 2017;136:e60–e122.

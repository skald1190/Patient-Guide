# Section 3: Cluster headache and other headaches

### About these conditions

**Cluster headache** causes attacks of severe pain around or behind one eye. The eye on that side may be red and watery, with a runny or stuffy nose or a droopy eyelid. Attacks often happen at the same time of day or night and can happen several times a day. They usually come in **"cluster periods"** that last weeks to months, followed by pain-free breaks. Treatment has two parts: fast relief for each attack, and a daily preventive medicine during the cluster period.

This section also covers two less common headaches:

- **Indomethacin-responsive headaches:** rare headache types that get better only with indomethacin.

- **RCVS (reversible cerebral vasoconstriction syndrome):** blood vessels in the brain tighten for a while and cause sudden, severe "thunderclap" headaches. It usually gets better over weeks to months.

Medicines in this section: oxygen, sumatriptan shot, verapamil, Emgality (cluster dose), indomethacin, nimodipine (for RCVS).

## Cluster headache plan {#cluster-headache-plan}

**When an attack starts, use oxygen or a sumatriptan shot.** At first, try one at a time to see which works best for you.

## Oxygen {#oxygen}

1.  Sit up and lean slightly forward.

2.  Put on the non-rebreather mask (the one with the bag).

3.  Turn the flow to **12 to 15 liters per minute.**

4.  Breathe normally for **15 minutes.**

- **Safety:** no smoking, candles, or open flames near the oxygen.

- **Pregnancy:** ✅ Generally OK · **Breastfeeding:** ✅ Generally OK

## Sumatriptan 6 mg shot {#sumatriptan-shot}

- Give 1 shot under the skin when the attack starts.

- You may give a second shot after **at least 1 hour**.

- **Never more than 2 shots in 24 hours.**

- **Common side effects:** chest or throat tightness, tingling, flushing. These usually pass quickly.

- **Call 911** if chest pain is severe or does not go away.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ✅ Generally OK

### Cluster headache prevention {applies=cluster-headache-plan,verapamil-3}

- [ ] Verapamil (see below)

- [ ] **Emgality for cluster:** 3 shots (100 mg each) on the same day at the start of a cluster period, then 3 shots monthly until the cluster period ends.

- This is different from the migraine dose.

- Allergic reactions (rash, swelling, trouble breathing) can happen days after the shot.

- **Pregnancy:** ⛔ **Avoid** (stop 5 to 6 months before trying) · **Breastfeeding:** ⚠️ Discuss with us

**Avoid alcohol during a cluster period.** It often triggers attacks.

- **Common side effects:** constipation (most common; eat fiber and drink water), ankle swelling, tiredness, dizziness.

- **Call us if:** you faint, your pulse is very slow, or you are short of breath.

- **Good to know:** **do not stop suddenly.** When your cluster period ends, we will lower the dose over 2 to 4 weeks. Verapamil interacts with many medicines (some cholesterol pills, gepants, lithium). Avoid grapefruit juice. Check with your pharmacist.

- **Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us (often the first choice for cluster prevention in pregnancy) · **Breastfeeding:** ✅ Generally OK

Always take with food. Take your stomach-protecting pill (PPI) every day while on indomethacin: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

| Week 1                                       | 25 mg, 3 times a day                          | ☐        |

- **Common side effects:** upset stomach, headache, dizziness, ankle swelling.

- **Go to the ER if:** you have black or bloody stools, or vomit blood or what looks like coffee grounds.

- **Avoid:** ibuprofen, naproxen, and aspirin while on indomethacin, unless we say it is OK.

- **Pregnancy:** ⛔ **Avoid from 20 weeks of pregnancy on** (it can harm the baby's kidneys and heart). Before 20 weeks: ⚠️ discuss with us. · **Breastfeeding:** ✅ Generally OK

## Nimodipine (for RCVS, a blood vessel tightening condition) {#nimodipine}

- **How to take:** My dose: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. Planned length: \_\_\_\_\_\_ weeks.

- **Common side effects:** low blood pressure, lightheadedness, headache, flushing. If you feel faint, skip that dose and call us.

- **Avoid while you recover:** migraine triptans, decongestants (Sudafed, phenylephrine), stimulants, cannabis, and grapefruit juice. Avoid heavy exercise, straining, and sex until the thunderclap headaches have stopped.

- **Call 911 if:** you have a new sudden severe headache, weakness, numbness, trouble speaking, vision changes, or a seizure.

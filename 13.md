# Section 13: Rare genetic conditions

### About these conditions

These conditions are caused by a change in a single gene. Each one has a treatment aimed at its specific cause.

- **SMA (spinal muscular atrophy):** weakness from loss of the nerve cells that control muscles. Risdiplam helps the body make more of a protein these nerve cells need.

- **Fabry disease:** a missing enzyme lets a fatty substance build up in the nerves, kidneys, heart, and skin. This can cause burning pain in the hands and feet. Treatment replaces the enzyme or helps your own enzyme work better.

- **Hereditary ATTR amyloidosis:** the liver makes an abnormal protein that builds up in the nerves and heart. This can cause numbness, pain, weakness, dizziness when standing, and stomach problems. Treatment lowers how much of this protein the liver makes.

- **Pompe disease:** a missing enzyme lets a sugar (glycogen) build up in the muscles. This causes weakness, especially in the hips and breathing muscles. Treatment replaces the enzyme.

Medicines in this section: risdiplam (Evrysdi) for SMA; Fabrazyme, Elfabrio, and Galafold for Fabry disease; Amvuttra, Wainua, and Onpattro for hereditary ATTR amyloidosis; Lumizyme, Nexviazyme, and Pombiliti plus Opfolda for Pompe disease.

**Pregnancy:** if you could become pregnant, talk with us and your specialist center **before** trying. Some of these medicines need birth control, and others may be continued with close monitoring.

## Hereditary ATTR amyloidosis with nerve damage {#attr-amyloidosis}

My medicine:

- [ ] **Amvuttra:** a shot every 3 months, given in clinic.

- [ ] **Wainua:** a shot you give yourself once a month in the belly or thigh. If you miss one, give it as soon as you can and restart the monthly schedule from that day.

- [ ] **Onpattro:** an IV infusion every 3 weeks, with medicines beforehand to prevent reactions.

#### Everyone on these medicines needs vitamin A:

- Take the daily recommended amount (about 2500 to 3000 IU a day). **Do not take more than this.**

- Tell us if you have trouble seeing at night or dry eyes.

- **Pregnancy:** vitamin A levels that are too low or too high can harm a baby. Tell us before trying to get pregnant.

Family members may want genetic counseling.

- **Amvuttra, Wainua, Onpattro: Pregnancy:** ⚠️ Discuss with us · **Breastfeeding:** ⚠️ Discuss with us

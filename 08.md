# Section 8: Multiple sclerosis (MS) symptoms

### About this condition

**Multiple sclerosis (MS)** is a condition where the immune system attacks the protective coating (myelin) around nerves in the brain, spinal cord, and eyes. This can cause numbness, weakness, vision problems, trouble walking, tiredness, bladder problems, and sensitivity to heat. Your **MS disease-modifying medicine** works to prevent new attacks and new damage (see **Part C**). The medicines in this section are different: they **ease day-to-day symptoms** like slow walking and fatigue.

Medicines in this section: dalfampridine (Ampyra), amantadine, modafinil.

Also see: Section 9 (stiffness and cramps), Section 15 (bladder), and Part C (MS disease-modifying medicines).

## Dalfampridine (Ampyra) (to help walking) {#dalfampridine}

- **How to take: 10 mg twice a day, about 12 hours apart.** This dose never changes. Swallow whole; do not split, crush, or chew.

- **Missed a dose? Skip it.** Never take extra or double up. More medicine raises seizure risk without helping more.

- **Kidney blood test** before starting and **at least once a year.**

- **Trial:** we will time your walk before starting and again in 2 to 4 weeks. If it is not helping, we stop it.

- **Common side effects:** bladder infections, trouble sleeping, dizziness, headache, nausea, balance problems.

- **Stop and call 911 if:** you have a seizure (do not restart), or hives, swelling, or trouble breathing.

- **Good to know:** this is **not** the same medicine as amifampridine (Firdapse).

- **Pregnancy:** ⛔ **Avoid** (not enough safety information; usually stopped before pregnancy) · **Breastfeeding:** ⚠️ Discuss with us

## Heat and MS {#heat-and-ms}

- Getting overheated can bring back old MS symptoms for a short time: blurry vision, weakness, tiredness, numbness. This is **not** a relapse. It goes away when you cool down.

- **Stay cool:** air conditioning, cooling vests or neck wraps, cold drinks, cool showers. Exercise in the early morning or evening. Avoid hot tubs and saunas.

- **Do not put cooling packs directly on bare skin.** They can cause cold burns.

- **Call us if:** symptoms last more than 24 hours after cooling down, or you have symptoms you have never had before. Also tell us about any fever or bladder infection symptoms.
